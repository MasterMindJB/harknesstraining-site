Save a seat Book a 20-minute demo

Learn. Practice. Feedback.

  • Learn. We start with why conversations get mishandled, then spend 5 to 10 minutes on a simple storytelling framework: character, goal, challenge, resolution.
  • Practice. One person runs a conversation they are dreading with an improv actor, briefed to push back the way the other person would: interrupting, doubting, going quiet.
  • Feedback. Each observer writes a 4×6 card: “When you said ___, I felt ___.” Then you run it again.

Open Lab is 90 minutes. If you'd rather watch, tick that when you save a seat. Keep the scenario general: no names, programs, customers, or export-controlled details. Sessions are recorded so you can see where the conversation turned.
