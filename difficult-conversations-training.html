The actor

Briefed to push back like the other person

The actors are improv actors. They interrupt, doubt, and go quiet. They can also get aggressive, change the subject, pull rank, or ask “Why now?” Pressure is where conversations get mishandled, so that is where we practice.

The room

Peer feedback, on a card

Most mishandled conversations go uncorrected because nobody tells you how your words landed. Here, each observer writes a 4×6 card: “When you said ___, I felt ___.” Then you go again while they watch.

We spend 5 to 10 minutes on character, goal, challenge, and resolution. Then the hot seat.

After

You can watch the turn

Sessions are recorded so you can see exactly where a conversation turned. Keep the scenario general and unclassified: no program names, customers, or export-controlled details.
