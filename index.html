    Questions

    Before you sign up

    Is this for me, or for my team?

    Both. A mishandled conversation costs the person who has it and the team around them. Individual leaders start at a free Open Lab and can join the monthly Practice Lab. Teams start with a free 20-minute demo or a Kickoff, then a Pilot or the Full Program.

    Do I need a conversation ready?

    It helps. Bring the one you keep putting off: that's usually the one most likely to go wrong. Keep it general, with no names, programs or customers. If you'd rather watch, tick “I'd rather watch this time” when you save a seat.

    Why would an engineer need this?

    At a certain level, the job is mostly conversations. A mishandled one can sink a good design, hide a risk until it's late, or cost you a good engineer. You were promoted for your technical judgment. Nobody trained you for the conversation where you have to defend it.

    What happens at an Open Lab?

    We start with why conversations get mishandled, then spend 5 to 10 minutes on a simple storytelling framework. Then the hot seat: one person runs a conversation they're dreading with an improv actor, the room gives feedback (“When you said ___, I felt ___”), and they run it again with the feedback.

    Who are the actors?

    Improv actors, briefed to push back the way the other person would: interrupting, doubting, going quiet. Pressure is where conversations get mishandled, so that's where we practice.

    Why practice with peers watching?

    Most mishandled conversations go uncorrected because nobody tells you how your words landed. Here, everyone does. Each observer writes a 4×6 card: “When you said ___, I felt ___.” Then you go again.

    Is it recorded? What about sensitive work?

    Sessions are recorded so you can see exactly where a conversation turned. Keep scenarios general and unclassified: no program names, customers or export-controlled details.

    Online or in person? What time?

    Both, because hard conversations happen on video and across the table. Every Open Lab has an online session at noon Pacific. Once a month there's also an in-person session in El Segundo at 6 PM Pacific. Team sessions run online, at your site, or in El Segundo.
