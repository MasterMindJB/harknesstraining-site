How it works

Pressure-tested with actors.
Validated by peers.

  1. Bring the real oneThe conversation you're dreading this month. Telling my VP the date slipped
  2. An actor pushes backInterrupts, doubts, goes quiet. “Why am I only hearing about this now?”
  3. Build a story CharacterGoalChallengeResolution You're the engineer this team leans on most. My goal is to ship Friday with something our customers can trust. The challenge is I can see you're burning out, and this is the third weekend in a row. So I'm asking for Saturday, and you take Monday and Tuesday off. Fair?
  4. Go againSame pushback. You hear the difference. Take 1“Ha, so… any chance you're free Saturday? Again?” Take 2“I know it's the third weekend. Here's why, and what you get back.”
